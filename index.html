06 / RESEARCH PROTOCOL

What this tracker does — and does not — measure

Hukou access is not a single variable. It involves registration, residency documentation, service eligibility and the practices through which eligibility is administered.

Full coding methodology ↗
01

Separate national ambition from local rules

National documents establish policy priorities; they are never counted as proof that a local eligibility condition has changed.

02

Distinguish law from implementation

Formal policy provisions are coded separately from measured uptake, access outcomes and implementation evidence.

03

Preserve unknowns

Without a verified primary document, a jurisdiction is marked awaiting verification, not ranked as restrictive or permissive.

04

Respect municipal variation

Provincial jurisdiction does not imply uniformity across cities, districts or individual service schemes.
